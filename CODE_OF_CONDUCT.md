# Code of conduct

Be decent to people. Criticise code, designs and decisions as sharply as you
like; do not criticise the person who wrote them.

Unacceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing
someone's private information, and persistent disruption after being asked to
stop.

Report a problem by opening a private security advisory or contacting the
repository owner directly. Reports are handled confidentially, and the
maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or block accounts.
